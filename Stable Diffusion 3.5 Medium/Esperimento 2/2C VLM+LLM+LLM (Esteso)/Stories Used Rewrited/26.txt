Full shot: Tom and Lily crouch low, hidden behind a small, thorny bush within the dense maze. Behind them, a massive, muscular lion roars loudly, its piercing predatory eyes fixed directly on their hiding spot. The danger is immediate.

Extreme close-up: Lily's face contorts with pain and terror as she lies in a narrow hole in the bushes. Her leg is clearly visible. The lion's sharp, powerful claws are just barely reaching into the hole, pinching her leg tightly. She is trapped.

Triumphant shot: Tom and Lily burst from the maze exit, running directly into the open arms of their mom and dad waiting outside. They embrace tightly, a group hug filled with immense relief and joy. They are finally safe.
