Blacksmith Torvin, a heavily muscled man with deep scars and intense eyes, hammers furiously on an anvil. His volcanic forge glows with overwhelming heat and flying sparks. Dramatic light emanates from the lava flow, highlighting his powerful form.

Torvin holds up a horrifying, perfectly smooth object: the mythical Heart of Stone. Made of glowing, forbidden meteorite fragments, the artifact pulses with an eerie light. His grim, focused expression reveals the intensity of its creation.

The tyrannical Duke, clad in fine, ornate armor, attempts to seize the glowing Heart of Stone. Torvin quickly touches the artifact. A brief, cold blue light flashes around his hand, momentarily illuminating the struggle.

Torvin stands victorious over the defeated Duke, his expression now stoic and utterly empty. He shows no emotion. In the background, Torvin's wife openly weeps, a single tear visible on her cheek.

Torvin brings his heaviest, glowing hammer down with full force onto the Heart of Stone. It shatters violently on the anvil. His body contorts in agonizing pain, his face twisting as warmth and vivid color rush back into him.
