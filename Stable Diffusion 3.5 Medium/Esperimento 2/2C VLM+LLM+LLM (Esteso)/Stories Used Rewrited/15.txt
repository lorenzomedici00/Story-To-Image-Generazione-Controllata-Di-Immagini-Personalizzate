A charismatic minstrel, Liana, cloaked and carrying a lute, traverses a vast, bleak, overcast tundra. Her wind-battered clothing and equipment show signs of a long, arduous journey across the desolate landscape. The emptiness stretches infinitely under a gray sky.

Liana stands at the base of a colossal, shard-like glacier spire. Within its frozen core, a powerful, ancient, malevolent spirit is vaguely visible, its shadowy form radiating cold and an eerie silence. The ice glistens ominously around her.

Close-up on Liana's lute. Its strings shatter into icy fragments, crumbling under a chilling, unseen force. Her bare hands, touching the instrument, are visibly freezing and turning numb from the intense, insidious cold. Sharp shards fall around her.

A close-up of Liana's face reveals frozen tears on her cheeks. Her mouth opens, emitting a slow, raw, wordless melody of profound sadness. Visible, warm musical notes stream from her lips, visibly melting the frigid air around her.

The colossal glacier rapidly melts, rivers of water cascading down. Liana, triumphant, captures a swirling vortex of vibrant light and ethereal music – the 'Frozen Song' – into her lute. A rush of vibrant color floods the formerly bleak, silent world.
