A young, naive boy stands apart, confused by tales of fear. He longs to understand "shuddering," believing it's a skill he must learn. His innocent face holds a determined look, set against a simple village backdrop. He practices an imagined "shudder," but feels nothing.

At midnight, the boy approaches a looming church tower, a heavy bell rope visible inside. He is sent by the sexton, who now hides on the dark stairs, shrouded in a white sheet, ready to act as a terrifying ghost. The scene is dark and spooky, with the boy's small figure contrasting the large tower.

The boy calmly faces the silent, sheet-clad figure by the bell-rope. Unfazed, he issues stern warnings. When the "ghost" remains still, the boy shoves him down the spiraling stairs. The sheet flies off as the sexton tumbles. The boy then confidently rings the massive bell.

Cast out, the boy wanders, still yearning to shudder. He enters a grim, ancient castle, its stones crumbling. Inside, he confronts fierce demonic cats with glowing eyes and snarling black dogs. He casually plays nine-pins with scattered bones and skull pieces, utterly fearless.

The victorious youth sleeps soundly in the king's castle, now married to the beautiful princess. She, with her maid's sly grin, pours a bucket of icy water mixed with small gudgeon fish over him. He leaps up, startled and drenched, finally experiencing a true, undeniable shudder.
