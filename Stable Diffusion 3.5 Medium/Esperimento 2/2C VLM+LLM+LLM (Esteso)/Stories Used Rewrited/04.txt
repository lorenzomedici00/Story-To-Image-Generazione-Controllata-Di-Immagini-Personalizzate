An old mother goat gathers her seven kids inside their cozy home. She points a hoof, cautioning them about the wicked wolf. She describes his rough voice and distinctive black feet, emphasizing they must not open the door to anyone else. The little kids listen solemnly, their wide eyes fixed on her.

A large, grey wolf knocks forcefully on the door, his rough voice demanding entry. The seven kids peer out, refusing him. Frustrated, the wolf soon returns, his voice feigned softer. He presses black paws against the window, but the clever kids spot them, keeping the door firmly shut, infuriating the predator.

The cunning wolf enters a bustling bakery, intimidating the baker to cover his black paws with white dough. Next, he threatens a frightened miller, forcing him to generously dust the dough-covered paws with bright white flour. His signature black feet are now completely disguised, appearing perfectly white.

The wolf calls out with a soft, gentle voice. The kids see his perfectly white paws through the window. Convinced, they joyfully open the door. The terrifying wolf lunges in, swiftly swallowing six little kids. In panic, the smallest goat scrambles to hide, squeezing into a tall, dark clock-case.

The mother goat returns to a chaotic home, finding only her youngest in the clock-case. She locates the sleeping wolf in a meadow, his belly distended. Bravely, she cuts him open, releasing her six children. They fill his stomach with heavy stones, then sew him back. The wolf later stumbles into a well, drowning.
