A beautiful princess, the king's youngest and most cherished daughter, frequently played near a cool, deep forest well, her favorite pastime being the tossing and catching of a gleaming golden ball. She sat under an old lime-tree, enjoying the solitude, but the joy was contingent upon keeping her beloved toy safe from the water's edge.

Suddenly, her golden ball did not return to her waiting hands; instead, it rolled over the edge and vanished into the dark, profound depths of the well, beyond sight. The princess was immediately overcome with despair, weeping and crying out with such distress that her sorrow might have moved a stone to pity.

A grotesque frog thrust its large, ugly head from the water and addressed the sobbing princess, offering to retrieve the ball. In exchange, the princess impulsively promised him her most precious possessions: clothes, jewels, and even her crown, but the frog demanded a promise of love and companionship.

The princess, secretly finding the frog's demand absurd and believing he could never leave the well, quickly agreed to let him be her play-fellow, dinner guest, and bed companion. With the promise secured, the frog retrieved the ball and tossed it onto the grass, but the overjoyed princess immediately snatched her toy and fled home, forgetting her promise.

The next day, a splish-splash sound announced the frog's arrival at the castle door, demanding entry and the fulfillment of the pact. The king, upon learning of the princess's broken word, commanded her to honor the promise, forcing the princess to reluctantly welcome the frog to her dinner table and eventually, to her own silken bed.
