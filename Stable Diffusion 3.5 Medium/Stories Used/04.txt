An old mother goat prepared to leave her home to forage in the forest, first gathering her seven little kids to deliver a solemn warning. She cautioned them meticulously about the wicked wolf, describing his rough voice and his distinctive black feet, and commanding them not to open the door to anyone but her.

It wasn't long before the wolf arrived, knocking and demanding entry, using a feigned voice. The clever kids immediately recognized his rough voice and refused him access, causing the wolf to storm away. Determined, the cunning predator bought chalk to soften his voice, returned, and tried again.

This second attempt failed too, as the kids spotted his black paws pressed against the window. Frustrated, the wolf sought out a baker to cover his feet in dough and then threatened a miller into dusting his paws entirely with white flour, successfully disguising his signature black coloring.

For the third time, the wolf called out, and when the kids asked to see his paws, they were white. Believing the deceitful creature to be their mother, they joyfully opened the door, only to be met with the terrifying sight of the wolf himself. In a moment of panic, six kids were quickly swallowed whole, but the youngest managed to hide safely inside the clock-case.

The mother goat returned to a scene of chaos, finding only her youngest child in the clock-case, who recounted the horror. The mother found the wolf asleep in the meadow, his stomach lumpily distended, and bravely cut him open to release her six children unharmed. They then filled the wolf's belly with heavy stones and sewed him back up, ensuring the clumsy brute would drown miserably when he later stumbled into the well to quench his thirst.
