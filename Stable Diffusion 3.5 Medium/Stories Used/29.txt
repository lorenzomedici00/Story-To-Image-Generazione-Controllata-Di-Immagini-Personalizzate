Once upon a time, there was a hunter. He lived in a small house near the woods. He had a dog named Spot. Spot was a fierce dog. They were best friends.

One day, the hunter and Spot went into the woods to find food. They saw a big bird in a tree. 

The hunter got ready to shoot. Spot barked loudly. The bird flew away. They did not catch the bird, but they were not sad.

The hunter and Spot played in the woods. They ran and jumped. They had lots of fun. 

Then, they went back to their small house. They were happy to be together. And they lived happily ever after.
