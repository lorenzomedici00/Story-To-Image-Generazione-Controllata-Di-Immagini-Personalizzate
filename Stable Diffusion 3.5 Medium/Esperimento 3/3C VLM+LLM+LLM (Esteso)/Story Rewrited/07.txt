A stern king presents a key to a distraught queen. She secretly whispers to Benjamin, who leads his eleven brothers fleeing deep into a dark forest. The queen then hoists a blood-red flag high on the castle tower. Benjamin, watching from the forest's edge, sees the ominous signal.

The twelve enraged brothers gather in the dark forest, swearing a grim oath against all maidens. They discover a decrepit, bewitched hut, its windows glowing faintly. Benjamin, the youngest, stays inside, diligently cleaning and cooking, while his older brothers disappear into the dense woods to hunt.

Ten years later, a beautiful girl with a golden star on her forehead discovers twelve old shirts and empty coffins within the castle. Gripping the shirts, she bravely journeys alone into the dark, winding forest. She finds the mysterious hut. Benjamin, inside, looks up, startled, recognizing his sister immediately.

Benjamin hides his sister, then convinces his brothers to spare her. A joyful reunion follows, filled with relief. Later, in the moonlit forest garden, the sister innocently plucks twelve white lily flowers. Instantly, the twelve brothers transform into black ravens, screaming as they ascend into the night sky, and the mysterious hut vanishes.

The mute queen, bound at a pyre in the kingdom courtyard, faces condemnation from a wicked queen mother as flames ignite. Suddenly, twelve black ravens swoop down, transforming mid-air into her human brothers. They swiftly extinguish the fire. The rescued sister, finally speaking, embraces her joyful king, telling her incredible story.
