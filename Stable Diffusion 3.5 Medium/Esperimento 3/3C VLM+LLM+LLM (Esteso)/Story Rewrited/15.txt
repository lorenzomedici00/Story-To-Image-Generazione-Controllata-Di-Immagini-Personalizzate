A charismatic woman, Liana, with a wind-battered cloak and lute, journeys across a desolate, empty tundra under an overcast sky. Her equipment suggests a long, arduous trek through the bleak landscape.

Liana stands at the base of a colossal, shard-like glacier spire. Within its frozen core, a powerful, malevolent spirit, vaguely visible, lurks. The air is frigid and still.

The spirit's curse manifests: Liana's lute strings shatter into sharp icy fragments. Her bare hands, clutching the instrument, rapidly freeze and become numb from the intense cold and contact.

With tears frozen on her cheeks, Liana sings a slow, raw, wordless melody of profound sadness. Warm, visible musical notes emanate from her mouth, shimmering and melting the frigid air around her.

The glacier rapidly melts as Liana captures the released 'Frozen Song' – a swirling vortex of light and music – into her lute. Vibrant color rushes back, transforming the formerly bleak, silent world.
