Blacksmith Torvin, a heavily muscled man with deep scars, hammers furiously on an anvil in his volcanic forge. Sparks fly from the red-hot metal. Overwhelming heat from surrounding lava casts a dramatic, fiery glow, illuminating his intense eyes and focused expression.

Torvin intensely holds up a perfectly smooth, horrifying object: the mythical Heart of Stone. It glows faintly with forbidden blue light, meticulously crafted from meteorite fragments. His grim, focused face reflects the immense power of the artifact he has just forged.

The tyrannical Duke, clad in opulent armor, lunges to seize the glowing Heart of Stone from Torvin's grasp. Torvin quickly touches the artifact. A brief, cold blue light flashes around his hand, visibly indicating he absorbs its emotionless power.

Torvin stands victorious over the defeated Duke, his expression utterly stoic and empty, devoid of all emotion. In the background, his distraught wife weeps openly, a single tear visibly tracing a path down her cheek, witnessing his transformation.

Torvin raises his heaviest, glowing hammer high. With immense force, he brings it crashing down onto the Heart of Stone, shattering it completely on the anvil. His body convulses in agonizing pain as warmth and empathy violently rush back into his soul.
