A beautiful princess, the king's cherished daughter, plays by a cool, deep forest well. She sits under an old lime-tree, joyfully tossing and catching a gleaming golden ball. Sunlight filters through the leaves, highlighting her elegant dress as she keeps her precious toy from the water's edge.

Suddenly, the golden ball slips from her grasp. It rolls swiftly over the well's edge, vanishing into the dark, profound depths. The princess watches in horror, then collapses to the ground, overcome with despair. She weeps uncontrollably, tears streaming down her distressed face.

A grotesque, ugly frog thrusts its large head from the well water, addressing the sobbing princess. It offers to retrieve her ball, demanding a promise of companionship. The desperate princess quickly agrees to let him be her play-fellow, dinner guest, and bed companion, barely looking at the creature.

With the promise secured, the frog retrieves the gleaming golden ball. He tosses it onto the green grass near the well. The overjoyed princess immediately snatches her beloved toy. Without a backward glance, she turns and flees rapidly from the well, forgetting her solemn promise to the waiting frog.

The next day, a splish-splash sounds at the castle door. The frog demands entry, insisting on the pact's fulfillment. The King, firm, commands his princess to honor her word. Reluctantly, she shares her dinner table with the frog, then lies in her silken bed, the small frog perched beside her on the pillow.
