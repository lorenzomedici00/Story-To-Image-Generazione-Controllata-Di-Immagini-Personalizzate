An old mother goat gathers her seven little kids inside their cozy, rustic home. She points emphatically, warning them about the wicked wolf. Her eyes are stern as she describes his rough voice and distinctive black feet, instructing them not to open the door to anyone but her. The kids listen intently.

A cunning wolf knocks loudly at the goat's sturdy wooden door, using a rough, feigned voice. The clever kids inside immediately recognize the sound and refuse entry, peering through a small window. Frustrated, the wolf later returns, attempting another entry, but the kids spot his black paws pressed against the windowpane.

Determined, the wolf visits a bustling baker's shop, getting his black paws thoroughly covered in sticky white dough. Afterwards, he threatens a dusty miller, who then liberally dusts the dough-covered paws with white flour. His signature black feet are now completely disguised, appearing perfectly white.

The wolf, with his new white paws, knocks at the door again. The kids, seeing the white paws through the window, mistakenly believe it's their mother. They joyfully unlatch the door. The terrifying wolf lunges inside, swiftly swallowing six screaming kids whole. The smallest kid quickly hides inside a tall, ornate clock-case.

The mother goat returns to chaos, finding only her trembling youngest child peeking from the clock-case. She soon discovers the wolf asleep in a sunlit meadow, his belly noticeably distended. Bravely, she cuts him open, releasing her six kids. They fill the wolf with heavy stones, sew him up, and he drowns miserably in a nearby well.
