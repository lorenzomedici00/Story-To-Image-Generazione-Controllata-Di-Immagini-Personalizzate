A simple peasant, dressed in worn clothes, walks along a dusty country road beside a sun-dappled pond. He carries a small coin pouch. Green frogs croak loudly from the water. The peasant stops, his face contorting in anger as he glares at the amphibians, believing they argue about his talers.

Enraged, the peasant loudly counts his seven silver talers, shaking the coins at the croaking frogs. Their persistent sound further infuriates him. With a furious shout, he hurls the entire pouch of money into the pond, creating a large splash. He stands defiantly, arms crossed, staring intently at the water's surface.

Days later, the peasant approaches a rustic town gate, struggling with large cuts of raw meat. A pack of street dogs, led by a sleek greyhound, barks aggressively. The peasant gestures towards the greyhound, then to the meat pile. Convinced, he confidently deposits the entire load of meat on the dusty ground for the waiting canines.

The distraught peasant, after being chased by a butcher with a broomstick, stands before an ornate royal throne. He recounts his absurd tales to a stern King. A young, elegant Princess, seated beside her father, suddenly bursts into spontaneous, hearty laughter, her face beaming with joy, a truly unprecedented sight.

The peasant, now shrewdly wearing a fine, oversized coat, stands smiling widely. An angry soldier and a dishevelled Jew, coat clearly missing, glower nearby, bearing marks of a beating. The amused King looks on as the peasant receives a substantial, final reward, securing his clever victory and the last laugh.
