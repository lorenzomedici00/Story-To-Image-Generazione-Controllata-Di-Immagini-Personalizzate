A wide shot shows Cartographer Seraphina in practical leather gear. She explores a massive, overgrown ruin of mossy stone and shattered columns. Seraphina clutches a cryptic, forgotten parchment in one hand and an antique brass compass in the other, intently searching the ancient site.

Seraphina kneels before a crumbling stone archway, her expression focused. She raises a smooth, glowing lens, carved from moonstone, to her right eye. Through the lens, a hidden, vibrant gate of shimmering light vibrates, clearly blocking the passage within the ancient ruin.

An Ethereal Guardian, a figure of silent, shimmering energy, manifests before Seraphina. She stands perfectly still, her eyes closed, deep in concentration. Her posture is rigid as she mentally performs a complex, unseen test, her mind engaged with the energy being.

Having passed the test, Seraphina steps through the now open, vibrant light gate. She enters the Unseen City, a stunning metropolis constructed entirely of pure, silent light and reflective glass structures. A profound, otherworldly glow surrounds her within this ethereal realm.

Seraphina stands back in the familiar ruins, her face illuminated by a newfound wisdom. She holds a newly drawn, complete, and highly detailed map of the Unseen City on heavy parchment. A faint, knowing smile graces her lips, marking her triumph.
