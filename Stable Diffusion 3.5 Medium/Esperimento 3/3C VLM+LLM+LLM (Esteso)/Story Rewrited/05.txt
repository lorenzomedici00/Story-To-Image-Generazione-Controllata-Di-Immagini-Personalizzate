An old king, frail in a grand bedchamber, summons his trusted servant, Faithful John. With a dying breath, he points to a forbidden, ornate door in a long palace gallery. He entrusts John with his young son's future, warning him against that hidden chamber and its dangerous secret.

Years later, the young Prince notices the forbidden, locked door in the grand gallery. He insists, despite John’s tearful warnings, that it be opened. Inside, a breathtaking portrait of a princess mesmerizes him. The Prince instantly faints onto the polished floor, overcome with passionate love for the depicted royal.

The young King commands John to win the princess. John oversees artisans crafting five tons of glittering gold into exquisite wares. Disguised as wealthy merchants, the King and John sail a grand ship. They lure the Princess of the Golden Dwelling aboard, showcasing their dazzling golden treasures to her, as the ship departs.

At the helm, John overhears three dark ravens on the mast, foretelling doom. He quickly stops the King from mounting a lethal chestnut horse. Later, he tears a poisonous bridal garment from the Queen. Finally, John intercepts the Queen, preventing her fatal swoon. With each life-saving act, John slowly turns to solid stone.

The King, distraught, stands before John's stone form. Repentant, he embraces his twin children, preparing to sacrifice them. Suddenly, John's stone body cracks, his flesh and life restored. He immediately revives the children. The King, Queen, children, and John embrace joyfully, a reunited and happy family on the ship's deck.
