A young brother and sister, looking sad, flee a gloomy house. They walk through a dense, sun-dappled forest until evening. Exhausted, they find refuge inside a large, ancient hollow tree. Moonlight filters through the canopy as they sleep soundly, nestled together.

The parched brother nears a sparkling forest stream, but his sister hears a warning whisper. They approach a second, then a third. Overcome by thirst, he drinks the clear water. Instantly, he transforms into a gentle roebuck. The heartbroken sister ties a golden garter and rushes around his neck, leading him to a secluded cottage.

A majestic royal hunt sweeps through the forest. The golden-collared roebuck runs amongst the deer, catching the King's eye. A watchful huntsman secretly follows the slightly wounded roebuck. He observes it stopping at a small, secluded cottage, uttering the password, and quietly entering through the rustic door.

The King, using the password, finds the beautiful girl and marries her in a grand palace. The roebuck stays by her side. Later, a wicked witch, disguised, suffocates the Queen in a dim bath. The witch's ugly, one-eyed daughter replaces the true Queen on the throne.

At midnight, the ethereal spirit of the true Queen appears in the royal bedchamber. She gently nurses her baby and strokes the watchful roebuck. On the third night, the hidden King embraces her, instantly restoring her to life. The wicked witch is burned on a pyre, and the roebuck magically transforms back into the young brother, forever human.
