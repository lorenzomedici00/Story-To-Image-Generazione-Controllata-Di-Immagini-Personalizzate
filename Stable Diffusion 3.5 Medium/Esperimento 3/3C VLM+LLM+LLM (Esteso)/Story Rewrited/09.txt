A frail, pregnant woman gazes longingly from her humble cottage at a magnificent, moonlit garden. Her desperate husband, eyes wide with fear, stealthily climbs over the high wall, his hands reaching for vibrant green rampion. As he plucks the herb, a cloaked, formidable enchantress appears abruptly behind him, her gaze piercing, her hand reaching out.

The terrified husband kneels before the stern enchantress, who stands tall and imposing amidst the garden's shadows. He pleads for his life, while she points an unyielding finger, demanding his newborn child. Later, the enchantress, with a cold expression, takes the tiny, swaddled baby Rapunzel from her weeping mother's arms, carrying her away.

A majestic, isolated stone tower pierces the sky, its single window high above. Inside, a young Rapunzel, with incredibly long, golden braided hair, gazes down. Below, the ancient enchantress calls out. Rapunzel unfastens her massive braids, which cascade down the tower wall. The old woman then grasps the thick hair, beginning her ascent.

A noble prince, riding his horse through a vibrant forest, halts, enchanted by a beautiful song echoing from the tall tower. He secretly watches the enchantress ascend Rapunzel's long hair. Later, the prince, repeating the secret words, climbs the golden tresses. He finds Rapunzel; they sit by the window, whispering conspiratorially, a silk ladder already partly woven.

An enraged enchantress violently cuts Rapunzel's golden braids with large shears. Rapunzel then stands, distraught, in a bleak, scorching desert. The prince, blinded and heartbroken, wanders through a desolate, thorny landscape. Years later, he is led to Rapunzel, who cradles two small children in a green oasis. Her tears of joy fall onto his eyes, instantly restoring his sight, revealing a tearful, reunited family.
