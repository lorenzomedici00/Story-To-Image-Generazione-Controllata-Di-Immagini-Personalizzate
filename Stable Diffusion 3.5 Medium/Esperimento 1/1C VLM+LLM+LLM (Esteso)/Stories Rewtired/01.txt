A beautiful young princess sits under an ancient lime-tree near a cool, deep forest well. Sunlight filters through the leaves onto her flowing gown. She smiles, tossing a gleaming golden ball high into the air, then catching it with graceful hands, always careful to keep it from the dark water's edge.

Suddenly, the golden ball slips from her grasp. It bounces once on the mossy ground, then rolls swiftly over the stone edge, vanishing into the well's profound, black depths. The princess cries out, collapsing to her knees in despair, tears streaming down her face, hands outstretched towards the dark water.

From the murky well water, a large, grotesque frog thrusts its ugly, warty head. Its bulging eyes fix on the weeping princess. It opens its wide mouth and croaks, offering to retrieve her lost golden ball in exchange for a promise of companionship and affection.

The princess, eyes wide with desperation, quickly agrees to the frog's unusual demand. The frog dives, then resurfaces, expertly tossing the shining golden ball onto the grass. Overjoyed, the princess snatches her treasured toy, then turns abruptly and flees through the dense forest, forgetting her solemn promise.

The next day, a wet frog sits on a grand dining table in the castle, facing a reluctant princess and a stern king. Later, the same frog lies on a silken pillow in the princess's lavish bed, its small body contrasting with the luxurious fabrics, as the princess watches, resigned.
