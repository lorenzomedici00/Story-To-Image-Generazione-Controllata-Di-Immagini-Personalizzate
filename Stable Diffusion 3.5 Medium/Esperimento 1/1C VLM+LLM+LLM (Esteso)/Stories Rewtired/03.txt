A young, puzzled boy watches villagers recoil in fear from a dark churchyard. He doesn't understand their "shuddering" reaction. Determined, he decides this must be a skill, an art, he needs to master.

A young boy, alone, climbs a gloomy church tower towards the bell-rope at midnight. On the dark, winding stairs, a figure cloaked in a white sheet stands motionless, a silent, ghostly presence awaiting his approach.

The boy, fearless, confronts the silent white figure. Unmoving, he shoves it forcefully down the dark, winding stairs. The 'ghost' tumbles violently, breaking a leg. Undeterred, the boy calmly grasps the bell-rope and begins to ring the heavy church bell.

Cast out, the boy journeys to a crumbling, haunted castle, determined to feel fear. Inside, he battles two glowing-eyed demonic cats and fierce black dogs. Later, in a grand hall, he calmly plays nine-pins, using old bones as pins, utterly without dread.

In a lavish royal bedroom, the sleeping youth, now a prince, still longs for fear. His beautiful wife silently pours a bucket of icy water mixed with wriggling gudgeons over him. He violently jolts awake, finally leaping up and crying out in genuine shock and shuddering.
