A scarred, muscular blacksmith, Torvin, stands before a tyrannical Duke in a shadowed, stone throne room. The Duke, cloaked and stern, gestures towards a menacing design scroll, commissioning Torvin to forge a mythical, emotion-draining Heart of Stone. Torvin's expression is grim, a heavy hammer visible nearby.

Torvin toils in his intense, volcanic forge. Molten metal glows fiercely, sparks fly as his hammer crashes down onto an anvil. He shapes the Heart of Stone, using shimmering, forbidden meteorite fragments. Sweat beads on his muscular brow, firelight reflecting in his determined eyes.

The finished, pulsating Heart of Stone rests on an anvil. The tyrannical Duke lunges to seize it with greedy hands. With lightning speed, Torvin touches the artifact first. A cold, empty aura envelops Torvin's face, his eyes suddenly devoid of warmth, as the stone’s power transfers.

Torvin, now expressionless and efficient, stands over the defeated Duke, who lies broken on the stone floor. Torvin’s gaze, cold and hollow, then falls upon his wife, weeping openly in a shadowed doorway. Her profound sorrow etches a flicker of confusion onto his emotionless face.

Back in his volcanic forge, Torvin raises his heaviest hammer high. He smashes the Heart of Stone on the anvil, shattering it into glowing dust. A wave of excruciating pain contorts his face, followed by a rush of profound human warmth and deep empathy returning to his soul.
