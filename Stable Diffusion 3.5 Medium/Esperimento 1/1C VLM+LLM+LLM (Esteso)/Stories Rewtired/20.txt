A little girl, Lucy, stands in her small, cozy house. She wears a vibrant orange dress. An orange cat purrs beside her, as she holds a bright orange ball. Everything around her reflects her love for the color orange, creating a warm, inviting atmosphere.

Lucy giggles, playing indoors with her new friend, a translucent, gentle spirit. The spirit hovers playfully, batting Lucy's bright orange ball with an unseen hand. They share a joyful moment, the spirit's form softly glowing as they play together.

A woman's voice calls, "Lucy, dinner!" Lucy pauses her play, holding the orange ball. She looks at the hovering spirit, gesturing, "Later?" The spirit, a shimmering, kind presence, nods its head gently, smiling softly in agreement as Lucy turns to leave.

At the dinner table, Lucy looks earnestly at her mother, who is cutting food. Lucy describes her spirit friend. Her mother, with a skeptical expression, shakes her head, saying, "Spirits aren't real." Lucy's face falls, her eyes showing a hint of sadness.

After dinner, Lucy rushes back to the same spot. The gentle spirit reappears, smiling brightly. Lucy throws her vibrant orange ball, and the spirit playfully bounces it back. They continue their joyful game, their connection clear and full of fun.
