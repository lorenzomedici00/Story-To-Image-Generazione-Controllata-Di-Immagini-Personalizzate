An old mother goat stands in her cozy cottage, seven small kids gathered around. She points her hoof, gravely warning them about the wicked wolf's rough voice and distinctive black feet. The little goats listen intently, wide-eyed, as she instructs them not to open the door for anyone else.

The wolf arrives at the cottage, knocking loudly. He speaks with a feigned, rough voice, demanding entry. The clever kids immediately recognize the deception and refuse access from inside. Frustrated, the cunning wolf buys white chalk to soften his voice, then returns, determined to try again at the cottage door.

The wolf returns, his voice now softened. He presses his distinctively black paws against the windowpane. The kids spot them, recognizing the trick, and again refuse entry. Furious, the wolf finds a baker to cover his feet in dough, then threatens a miller into dusting his paws entirely with white flour.

With his paws disguised white, the wolf calls out, and the kids, fully deceived, joyfully open the door. The terrifying wolf bursts in, quickly swallowing six panicked kids whole. The youngest goat, however, scrambles unseen into a tall wooden clock-case, hiding safely from the predator's gaze.

The mother goat returns to chaos, finding only her youngest child in the clock-case, who recounts the horror. She discovers the wolf sleeping in the sunny meadow, his stomach hugely distended. Bravely, she cuts him open with scissors, releasing her six children unharmed. They fill his belly with heavy stones and sew him up. The clumsy wolf later tumbles into a deep well and drowns.
