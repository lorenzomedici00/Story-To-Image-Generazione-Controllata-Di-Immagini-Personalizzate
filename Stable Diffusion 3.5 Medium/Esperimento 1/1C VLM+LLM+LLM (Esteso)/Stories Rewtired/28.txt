Lucy points a finger, carefully counting the numerous flowers on the tree. Her concentration breaks as she spots a delicate white powder dusting a vibrant blossom. Her eyes widen slightly, displaying clear curiosity.

Lucy gently touches the soft white powder with her fingertip. She turns to her mom, holding out her hand to show the mysterious substance. Mom kneels, smiling kindly, and points back to the flower, beginning to explain.

A bright smile blossoms on Lucy's face, her eyes sparkling with new understanding. With renewed interest, she resumes carefully counting the colorful flowers on the large tree, now appreciating their special powder.

Lucy, holding her mom's hand, walks along a sunny park path. The large, flower-covered tree is visible behind them. Lucy glances back, her face thoughtful and content, having learned a new secret of nature.
