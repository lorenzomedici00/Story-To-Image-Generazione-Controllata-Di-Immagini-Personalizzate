Tom and Lily, cheerful twins, stand before a vast green bush maze in a sunny park. Eagerly, they run inside, following the initial winding paths deeper into its green walls.

Deep within the maze, the twins appear lost amidst tall, leafy bushes. A terrifying roar echoes nearby. Suddenly, a massive, angry lion emerges from the foliage, its eyes fixed on them, stalking menacingly.

Panic-stricken, Tom and Lily spot a small, dark hole hidden in the dense bushes. They quickly crawl inside, hiding. The roaring lion approaches, sniffs the opening, and reaches a large, clawed paw deep within.

The lion's sharp claws pinch Lily's leg, making her cry out in pain. Brave Tom grabs a fallen stick. He quickly pokes the lion's eye, causing the beast to howl loudly and recoil, hurt and afraid.

Seizing their chance, Tom and Lily dash out of the maze's entrance. Outside, their relieved mom and dad embrace them tightly. The twins, safe and happy, hold their parents, recounting their adventure.
