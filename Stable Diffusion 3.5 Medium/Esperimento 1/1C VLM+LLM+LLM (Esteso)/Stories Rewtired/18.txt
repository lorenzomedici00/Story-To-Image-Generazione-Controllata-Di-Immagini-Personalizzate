A small boy, Tim, enters a sunny park. Green grass stretches around, with tall trees in the background. He spots a large, orange and black striped tiger sitting calmly near a vibrant bush, its eyes soft.

Tim laughs joyfully, throwing a red ball. The magnificent tiger pounces playfully, batting at the ball with its massive paw. They roll on the soft grass under the warm sun, having endless fun in the verdant park.

Suddenly, the big tiger begins to shake violently, its striped fur rippling and blurring. Tim freezes, his playful smile vanishing. His eyes widen in fear and confusion, watching the tiger's strange, unsettling transformation.

In a flash of light, the trembling tiger completely transforms. Now, a friendly, medium-sized golden retriever sits where the tiger once was. Tim stares, his mouth agape in utter surprise at the sudden, magical change.

Tim and his new dog friend chase each other, happy barks filling the air. As the sun sets, casting long shadows, Tim walks home. He holds a leash, with the happy golden retriever trotting faithfully beside him.
