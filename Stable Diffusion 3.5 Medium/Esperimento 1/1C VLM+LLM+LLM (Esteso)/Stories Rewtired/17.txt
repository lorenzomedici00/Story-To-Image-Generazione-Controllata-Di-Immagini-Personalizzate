Ollie, a brown otter, swims playfully with two other otters in a sunny, clear river. They dive and surface among smooth river stones and green aquatic plants. Sunlight glints on the water's surface as they enjoy their family time.

Ollie's mom, a larger otter, gestures towards the river. Ollie dives purposefully into the flowing water, a determined expression on his face. He swims swiftly through underwater reeds, searching for fish.

Ollie surfaces next to a yellow duck floating calmly on the water. They exchange quick greetings near a mossy riverbank. Ollie explains his need to catch fish, then dives back down, leaving the duck watching.

Underwater, Ollie spots a large, perfectly smooth, iridescent stone on the riverbed. He carefully grasps the gleaming, rainbow-hued object with his paws. The stone sparkles brightly, contrasting with the dark riverbed.

Ollie presents the beautiful, radiant stone to his excited family on the riverbank. His mom and two other otters gather closely, looking at the shiny object with wide smiles. They all admire the captivating stone, expressions of pure joy on their faces.
