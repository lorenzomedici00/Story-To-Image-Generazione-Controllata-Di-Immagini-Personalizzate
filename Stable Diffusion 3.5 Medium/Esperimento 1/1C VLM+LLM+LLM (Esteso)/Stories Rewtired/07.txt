A stern king displays twelve small, empty coffins and keys to his heartbroken queen in a grand castle chamber. The queen whispers urgently to young Benjamin. Soon, the twelve boys, led by Benjamin, flee through a dark, ancient forest, their faces anxious. A blood-red flag appears on the castle's highest tower, signaling danger.

In the deep, shadowed forest, the twelve enraged brothers see the red flag. They stand defiantly, swearing a solemn oath against maidens. They discover a gnarled, bewitched hut. Inside, Benjamin, now older, diligently keeps house by a crackling fire. His eleven brothers, carrying bows and hunted game, return to their dark, hidden dwelling.

Ten years later, a beautiful maiden with a golden star on her forehead stands distraught amidst twelve empty coffins and old shirts in the silent castle. Determined, she carries the shirts and treks through the same dense forest. She arrives at the gnarled, bewitched hut. Inside, Benjamin, now a young man, opens the door, gasping in recognition.

Benjamin quickly hides his sister as the eleven brothers return. They embrace her warmly after Benjamin's plea. Later, in a vibrant forest garden, the sister innocently plucks twelve pristine white lilies. Instantly, the flowers transform into twelve dark ravens, cawing as they ascend into the sky. The mysterious hut fades away, leaving her alone.

Years pass. The beautiful, silent queen, wearing a simple crown, sits beside her loving king in a grand castle. The king’s wicked mother whispers sinisterly, pointing at the queen. Soon, the queen is tied to a burning stake. Just as flames ignite, twelve black ravens swoop down, transforming into men, extinguishing the fire. The queen speaks, telling her joyous king her story.
