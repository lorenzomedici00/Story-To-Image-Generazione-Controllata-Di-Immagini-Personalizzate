Minstrel Liana, a fiery-haired woman with a worn lute, treks across a desolate, snow-covered tundra. Her cloak whips in the biting wind. Jagged, dark rocks pierce the icy ground under a grey sky. She moves purposefully, her gaze fixed on the distant horizon.

Liana reaches a colossal, gleaming spire, entirely encased in ancient, blue ice. The spire dominates the bleak landscape, radiating an unnerving stillness. Dark, shadowy fissures within the ice hint at a trapped, malevolent presence. She approaches cautiously, her determination visible.

Liana attempts to play her ornate lute. Instantly, an invisible force, like a tangible silence, lashes out. The lute strings snap violently, fraying into frozen wisps. Her fingers, numb and white with frost, seize up on the instrument's neck. Her expression is strained.

Discarding the shattered lute, Liana closes her eyes. She begins to sing, a raw, wordless melody of profound sorrow emanating from her. Ethereal, shimmering waves of sound visibly push back against the oppressive silence. A shadowy, anguished form stirs deep within the ice spire, reacting.

The shadowy spirit weeps visibly, its sorrow melting the ancient glacier rapidly. A swirling, luminous orb—the Frozen Song—emerges. Liana gently catches the orb. The world instantly explodes with vibrant colors and light, music flowing freely across the now lush, green landscape.
