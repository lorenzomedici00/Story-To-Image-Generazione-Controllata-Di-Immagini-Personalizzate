A pale, pregnant woman gazes longingly at a vibrant, walled garden. Her fearful husband, under moonlight, quickly snips green rampion leaves. Suddenly, a powerful, dark-cloaked enchantress appears. Her piercing eyes fix on the terrified man, seizing his arm amongst the garden's rich foliage. He trembles, dropping the stolen herbs.

The terrified husband kneels before the formidable enchantress, agreeing to her dreadful terms. Later, a newborn baby girl cries softly in a simple wooden cradle. The enchantress, cloaked and severe, materializes in the humble room, gently but firmly lifting the infant, "Rapunzel," from her bed, disappearing into the shadows.

A beautiful girl, Rapunzel, with exceptionally long, golden braids, gazes from a high, sole window of a doorless stone tower nestled in a dark forest. Below, the old enchantress calls up. Rapunzel unfastens her magnificent tresses, allowing the thick, gleaming hair to cascade down the tower wall for the enchantress to ascend.

A prince on horseback rides through the forest, pausing to hear a beautiful song emanating from the tower's window. Hidden, he observes the enchantress ascending Rapunzel's golden hair. Later, the prince repeats the call, climbs the long braids, and gently enters the tower room, meeting Rapunzel. They whisper, plotting escape.

An enraged enchantress violently shears Rapunzel's golden braids, banishing her to a desolate desert. The prince climbs, then plunges from the tower onto thorns, piercing his eyes. Blind, he wanders for years. Finally, he finds Rapunzel and their twin children in a green clearing. Her joyous tears restore his sight.
