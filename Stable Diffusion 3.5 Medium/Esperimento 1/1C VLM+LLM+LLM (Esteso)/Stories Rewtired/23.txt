Tom spots a tiny, sad fish near his boat's hull, its fins drooping. He leans over with a kind expression. The fish, suddenly joyful, leaps gracefully from the calm sea, landing safely inside Tom's large wooden boat.

Tom's boat glides smoothly over the sunny, calm ocean. The little fish is visible in a small, contained area within the boat, perhaps a shallow pool of water. They journey together, a friendly sailor and his small passenger.
